Pricing

Every plan includes the full app: on-device transcription, your private library, search, summaries, notes, Ask and everything in Create. Plans differ only in how much you can transcribe each month.

What counts toward my plan?

Only the length of recordings you transcribe. Opening, searching, summaries, notes, Ask, study tools and exports never use your time.

When does my time reset?

Free resets on the 1st of each month (UTC). Paid plans reset when your subscription renews each month.

What if I cancel or downgrade?

Your paid plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Your recordings, notes and everything you created stay in your library; only new transcription is limited.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Transcription runs on your device. Your plan only uses the length of each recording, never its audio, and Stripe only handles your payment details.

Payments are handled by Stripe · SparkScribe never sees your card · audio is never uploaded
