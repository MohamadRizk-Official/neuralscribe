Questions

What counts toward my plan?

Only the length of recordings you transcribe. Opening recordings, searching, summaries, notes, Ask, Create tools and exports never use your time.

When does my time reset?

Free resets on the 1st of each month (UTC). Paid plans reset when your subscription renews each month.

What happens if I cancel or downgrade?

Your paid plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Your recordings, notes and everything you created stay in your library; only new transcription is limited.

What leaves my device?

Never your audio: transcription runs on your device. Transcripts you save are stored privately in your account, and when you use an AI feature only the transcript text it needs is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic. Payments are handled by Stripe; SparkScribe never sees your card.
