    • Your audio stays on your device. Transcription runs in your browser; the recording is never uploaded.
    • AI features only see the text they need. When you use Summary, Notes, Ask or Create, the transcript text (never audio) goes to our AI provider, Anthropic.
    • Your library is yours. Saved transcripts and results are private to your account. Please only record or upload what you have permission to use.
