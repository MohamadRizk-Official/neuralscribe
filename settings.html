Mascot

The little crystal companion that reacts while you work.

  • On Full personality: it moves around, explores and reacts.
  • Quiet Stays in its corner. Only reacts to what matters: processing, a finished transcript, errors, Ask, Quiz and your clicks.
  • Off Hidden everywhere.

SparkScribe always follows your device’s “Reduce motion” setting.

Privacy

  • Your audio never leaves this device. Transcription runs in your browser.
  • Saved transcripts are private to your account. You can delete them any time in My Library.
  • AI features only send transcript text. Summary, Notes, Ask and Create send the transcript text (never audio) to our AI provider, Anthropic, only when you use them.
